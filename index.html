Know where your money goes. Just type it.

Add it to your home screen

Android

  1. Open this page in Chrome and tap Create a free account.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu, then Add to Home screen or Install app.
  3. Open Smart Spend from its new icon. It runs full screen and works offline.

iPhone and iPad

  1. Open this page in Safari and tap Create a free account.
  2. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  3. Open Smart Spend from its new icon. It runs full screen and works offline.
